# Eldervale — The Hollow Sun / Store campaign

English campaign, built from the existing v1.6.0 game. The emotional hook is memory loss and the search for Lyra. No ending spoilers. Midnight navy, warm ivory, antique gold. Large editorial typography; actual gameplay remains the central evidence.

## Deliverables
- Opaque square symbol artwork, 512 × 512 Google Play icon.
- 1024 × 500 feature graphic, original layered composition and source art.
- Eight 1080 × 1920 portrait screenshot compositions, with original unedited gameplay captures alongside them.
- Forty-second 1920 × 1080 and 1080 × 1920 trailers, English spoken narration, Foley and quiet ambience. No music or instruments.
- Editable Remotion project, asset provenance, copy and alt-text manifest, packaged exports.

## Screenshot sequence
1. A world worth remembering — Elderbrook exploration.
2. Make every strike count — actual sword combat.
3. Shape your strength — character talents and progression.
4. Follow the light — dungeon exploration.
5. Take the longer road — Frosthaven exploration.
6. She was real. Prove it. — Mara's opening dialogue.
7. Find your next advantage — actual equipment inventory.
8. A journey beyond the main quest — quest journal.

## Visual integrity
Cover art is clearly separated from real game footage. No invented game UI, ratings, awards, store availability or multiplayer claims. Illustrated people remain masked, hooded or back-facing; small in-game sprites follow the user's explicit exception. The new icon contains no living creature. Test fixtures affect isolated capture saves only, not player saves or shipped gameplay.
