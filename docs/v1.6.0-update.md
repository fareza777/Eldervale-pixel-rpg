# Eldervale v1.6.0 — controls, encounters and combat preferences

Android version code 8. The existing signing key, campaign format and previous
versioned release packages are preserved.

The left thumb summons a floating joystick at the touch position. Its origin
follows long drags, allowing quick reversals without returning across the
screen. Release, cancellation, lost capture, resizing, pauses and backgrounding
clear input. Movement and Attack retain independent pointer ownership.

Four compact Settings pages provide Audio, Controls, Combat and Comfort.
Controls include floating/fixed joystick, 96/108/120 px joystick size,
65–100% visibility and optional full-drag running. Combat includes aim assist,
Classic/Whirl sword combo, Subtle/Full impacts and Story/Adventure/Heroic.
Comfort includes reduced motion, camera shake and Android haptics. Older
preferences inherit the new defaults, and every choice persists locally.
Reset preferences does not modify the journey.

The normal sword combo uses directional strikes with a stronger third hit.
Whirl finisher enables the third-hit spin and surrounding sweep. Native asset
animations and frame-timed damage are retained. The signature skill has its
own animation independently of the basic combo preference.

Authored roads now contain spaced encounter groups: up to 42 enemies in
wilderness areas, 44 in long dungeon halls and 18 in guardian antechambers.
The placement pass validates walkable ground and leaves safe arrival pockets,
quest interactions and guardian courts clear. Towns and homes remain safe.
Enemies patrol near their home position and increase in strength with the
region level. Guardian reveals remain at the later arena threshold.

Every outdoor house now has a foundation collision footprint, including
abandoned houses along the Lantern Road, Broken Causeway and Pilgrim’s Ridge.
Existing town door positions remain accessible.

Hit feedback combines a short directional stroke, bright contact point,
class-colored shards, grounded recoil, brief stagger and collision-aware
knockback. Subtle/reduced effects limit the burst to two shards. No enemy
attack circles were restored.

Battle and inventory art use a new transparent 4×3 atlas. The original is
preserved. See [battle-icons-v2.md](battle-icons-v2.md) for the imagegen prompt
and workspace asset path.

Seven near-silent nature loops replace the earlier field-recording masters.
Three reusable ElevenLabs sound effects were requested as natural sheltered
air, stone-room air and distant water, explicitly excluding instruments,
music, birds, crackle and static. The final masters have RMS ≤ .008, peaks
< .056 and closed loop seams. Nearby combat smoothly reduces ambience to
22%, then restores it after danger. Foley and the 75 spoken performances
retain their independent volume controls.
