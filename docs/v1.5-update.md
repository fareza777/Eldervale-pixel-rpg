# Eldervale v1.5.0 — world, combat and full-screen Android

This update compares the read-only Dawnbound reference at `E:\Roguelike Opus 55`
with Eldervale's existing implementation. It preserves progression, content,
saves, nature-only audio, voices and the official AdMob test configuration.

## Playing space

Gameplay has no permanent bottom navigation bar. A 44px top-right button opens
four labeled shortcuts. Android Back closes that tray first. Choosing a panel,
moving or attacking closes it. Combat controls sit lower, with their previous
readable labels and two-thumb spacing. Native Android hides system bars while
playing and restores them for menus. The world extends behind cutouts while
interactive HUD elements keep their safe padding. Native inset handling preserves
keyboard resize on older WebViews and reserves banner space above Android navigation.
The implementation follows Android's [edge-to-edge](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/layout/edge-to-edge)
and [immersive mode](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/layout/immersive) guidance.

The world has the same readable two-CSS-pixel sprite scale. A capped 2x backing
canvas improves precision on high-density phones. Camera following uses fractional
world positions, time-adjusted smoothing, modest directional lead and southern
slack so the hero stays above the thumb controls. The render target is 60fps;
actual frame rate still depends on device hardware.
Scene and animation clocks use elapsed frame time rather than Phaser's averaged
delta, which could stretch movement and strike duration on a busy device.
The movement step still caps long stalls to avoid a sudden position jump.

## Combat

The six-frame native sword clip and original bow, throw and spin clips drive
their impacts through animation events. Completing a clip also resolves a
pending strike, preventing slow frames from losing or duplicating damage.
Dodge cancels pending hits; region changes cancel old actions. Held attacks
wait for the active animation instead of cutting it off.

Sword crescents, small impact bursts, brief enemy flashes, ground-anchored recoil
and decaying knockback make a connection visible. Heavy strikes have a short,
throttled impact pause. Arrows and magic use swept collision between frames.
The melee signature skill gains a rotating arc, and nova visuals match their
damage radius. Reduced-motion and camera-shake settings remain available.

## World direction

Six towns gain composed squares and door courts: planted islands and a lantern
well, canal crossings and an archive gate, winter monuments, and copper workshops.
Existing road and quest connections remain intact. Wild roads have grouped
foliage, roadside remains and regional landmarks. Dungeon chambers gain material
variation, raised wall faces, shadowed edges, broken inlays, regional emblems,
archive shelving and themed monuments. Water has complete banks and a single
quiet, batched reflection layer.

Original Super Retro cobblestones replace the flat repeated town bricks.
Standing furniture and flora retain their crisp source density and ground anchors.
The four generated landmark sprites contain architecture only and are reused
from one transparent atlas. Asset provenance and the generation prompt are in
[world-art-v1.md](world-art-v1.md).

Version: **1.5.0 / Android code 6**. Previous v1.4 signed files remain in `releases/`.
New APK and AAB use the existing upload key. Live billing configuration is still
required for real Remove Ads purchases; test ad IDs remain in use as requested.
