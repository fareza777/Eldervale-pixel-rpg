Eldervale · Action RPG

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Eldervale: Action RPG is an offline, single-player mobile game published by the developer shown on its Google Play listing. This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Information handled

Your story progress, settings, and inventory are saved locally on your device. Eldervale does not require an account and the developer does not operate a game server for player profiles, chat, or analytics.

The Android build may show optional advertising through Google Mobile Ads. Google may process device or advertising identifiers, approximate region, diagnostics, and ad interaction data to deliver, measure, and protect ads. You can manage available consent choices in the game's privacy controls and your Android or Google settings.

Optional purchases, including Remove Ads, are processed by Google Play. The developer receives purchase status from Google Play to unlock the entitlement and does not receive your payment card details.

Sharing and retention

Data handled by Google services is governed by Google's own policies. The developer does not sell personal information. Local saves remain on your device until you delete the game or its data; exported saves are controlled by you.

Children

Eldervale is a general-audience fantasy game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the game's services change. The current version will remain available at this URL. For support, use the contact option on the Eldervale Google Play listing.
