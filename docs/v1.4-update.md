# Eldervale v1.4.0

Hero motion now uses controlled acceleration and short braking. Warden and
Arcanist walk at 48 world pixels/second; Ranger walks at 54. Outer-joystick
running is 1.5 times walking speed. Earlier movement used 86/100 base speed and
a 1.6 run multiplier. Keyboard preview input walks. Diagonals are normalized,
collisions stop blocked velocity, and menus cancel motion. Animation cadence
follows velocity. Dodge keeps its native animation and resets motion inertia.

The repeated dots beside the hero were the ground quest route overlay. They
are removed. The compact quest compass and Journal objective remain, including
directions to moving NPCs.

All seven ambience loops have a quieter field-recording master: soft wind for
forests, river recordings near water, lower filtered wind for colder/interior
locations. The dedicated birds recording layer is excluded. Six-second joins,
lower peaks and no added tonal/instrumental layer keep the sound restrained.
Foley and all 75 spoken performances are preserved. Exact measurements and
provenance: `docs/quiet-ambience.json` and the audio `sound-manifest.json`.
`tools/master-quiet-ambience.py` reproduces loops from cached source recordings.

Test AdMob and Remove Ads integration: `docs/monetization.md`. Version is
1.4.0 / Android code 5. Existing saves and the upload key remain compatible.

Supply cooldown text updates while Support stays open and the button becomes
available again when its timer expires. The menu keeps its focus while updating.
