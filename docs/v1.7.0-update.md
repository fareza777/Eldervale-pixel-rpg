# Eldervale v1.7.0 — guardians, windups and lantern light

Version 1.7.0 / code 9 focuses on the two weakest parts of the campaign:
combat readability and the flat presentation of enclosed spaces.

## Gameplay

- **Guardians scale with their region.** Bosses previously kept their base
  attack and health while the player's defence grew with every level, so the
  Hollow King could be reduced to the two-damage floor. Bosses now follow the
  same per-region growth as ordinary enemies (health +16% and attack +10% per
  region level), and defence can never remove more than three quarters of a
  blow.
- **Enemy attacks are readable.** Every windup now draws a growing amber ring
  under the attacker for its full 650 ms (850 ms for guardians). The ring is
  cleared when the strike lands, when a heavy hit staggers the enemy, or when
  the enemy dies.
- **The Heal button spends the right supply.** It now chooses a Hearth Elixir
  for large wounds, a Healing Draught for small ones, and Aether Dew or
  Starlit Dew when health is full but mana is missing. The count on the button
  follows the item it will actually use.

## Visual

- Interiors and dungeons draw their floors and furnishings in half-light, so
  the existing lantern glows carve out real contrast instead of sitting on an
  evenly lit room.
- Each biome now carries drifting motes: leaves in the forest, spores in the
  marsh, snow in the ice fields and embers elsewhere. They use the small FX
  textures that were previously generated but never shown, and respect the
  reduced-motion setting.
- The vignette reacts to danger: it deepens below 30% health and again while a
  guardian is engaged.
- The hero renders on an integer pixel grid again, and the large landmark
  illustrations use linear filtering so they stop shimmering.

## Clean-up

- Removed the unused `routeInk` graphics object, its 650 ms clear tick and the
  legacy layout constants.
